02 — Signal Intelligence

Seven Signal
Categories.

Not every keyword match is a lead. LaunchLens isolates seven types of high-intent evidence from public conversations — then scores each with a transparent, explainable model.

Pain
Pain Signal
Direct expressions of friction or suffering with the current state of a workflow or tool.
"I spend hours reading documentation every week."
Intent
Intent Signal
Active searching for solutions — asking for tool recommendations or evaluating alternatives.
"Is there a tool that can automatically summarize this?"
Frustration
Frustration Signal
Emotional venting about workflow inefficiency — a strong leading indicator of switching intent.
"Our current workflow is painfully slow."
Switching
Switching Signal
Explicit signals of willingness to move away from a current tool or workflow.
"Looking for an alternative to our current tool."
